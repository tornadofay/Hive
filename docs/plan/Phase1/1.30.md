# 1.30 — Metrics, Budget Cap & OpenTelemetry

## Purpose

Establish V1 resource governance and observability across provider/model calls, Agents, runtimes, WorkItems, and consequential operations without exposing secrets.

## Scope

- Record request, success, failure, timeout, cancellation, and applicable latency metrics.
- Correlate provider/model activity with Agent, RuntimeInstance, Execution, and WorkItem identities without putting secret values in telemetry.
- Track applicable token/cost information from provider responses or configured pricing evidence.
- Enforce a hard per-runtime budget before and during applicable work.
- Respect applicable provider/model quotas and rate limits as operational constraints distinct from model-scoped limits.
- Produce OpenTelemetry traces/metrics through the established observability boundary.
- Ensure cancellation and budget exhaustion terminate work through normal Hive/MAF lifecycle paths.
- Redact credentials, authorization secrets, raw API keys, and other sensitive material from logs, metrics, trace attributes, and failure details.
- Keep telemetry observational; it must not grant capability, authorization, budget, or host-operation permission.

## Architecture boundary

Hive owns V1-specific budgets, safety, diagnostics, and audit semantics. MAF remains responsible for framework execution mechanics where applicable. Provider/model discovery remains evidence; pricing/limits and quotas must not be conflated.

## Non-goals

No second telemetry platform, secret-bearing diagnostic channel, automatic budget bypass, or new orchestration engine is introduced.

## Verify

Verify budget-stop behavior, provider/model and WorkItem correlation, cancellation propagation, timeout/failure metrics, deterministic redaction of secret material, OpenTelemetry trace/metric emission, and enforcement when provider/model operational limits are reached.
