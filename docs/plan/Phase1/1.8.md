# 1.8 — Outbox Poller

## Purpose

Process committed outbox records after their triggering transaction has successfully committed.

## Scope

- Poll durable unhandled outbox records through the Hive persistence boundary.
- Process each committed record through the owning handler/capability path.
- Mark delivery state only according to the durable outbox contract.
- Make duplicate delivery safe because a crash or retry can occur around processing.
- Recover work that was committed before the process stopped but had not yet been handled.
- Preserve correlation/provenance information from the originating durable state change.
- Keep processing cancellation-aware and lifecycle-safe.

## Architecture boundary

The outbox poller is a delivery mechanism, not a second source of business truth and not a workflow engine. Hive's durable state remains authoritative; the poller continues work after commit.

## Non-goals

No arbitrary external message broker, hidden in-memory-only queue, or new orchestration system is introduced.

## Verify

Verify committed records are processed, duplicate delivery is harmless, a crash before processing leaves the work recoverable, already-completed records are not incorrectly reprocessed as new work, and cancellation/disposal does not corrupt delivery state.
